AI Fight Arena - Multi-GPU Benchmark

Hardware:
2x NVIDIA L40S

Software:
PyTorch 2.11.0
CUDA 12.8
NCCL

MULTI-GPU TRAINING - PyTorch DistributedDataParallel

1 GPU throughput:       76,343 samples/sec
2 GPU throughput:       80,283 samples/sec
Speedup:                1.05x
Scaling efficiency:     52.6%

The DDP benchmark used one process per GPU, NCCL communication,
DistributedSampler, and synchronized gradient updates.

MULTI-GPU INFERENCE

1 GPU throughput:       525,155 samples/sec
2 GPU throughput:       1,023,350 samples/sec
Speedup:                1.95x
Scaling efficiency:     97.4%

The inference benchmark distributed independent inference workloads
across two NVIDIA L40S GPUs and measured aggregate throughput.
